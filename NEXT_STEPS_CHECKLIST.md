## 🤝 NEGOTIATION TACTICS

### If They Say It's Too Expensive:

**Response Option 1: Value Reframe**
*"I understand the investment is significant. Let's look at it this way: Jinay is pursuing scholarships that range from ₹50,000 to ₹5,00,000. A professional portfolio increases success rates by 40%. This website could literally pay for itself with just one scholarship or internship opportunity."*

**Response Option 2: Break It Down**
*"Let's break down the cost per feature. You're getting [X features] over [X weeks]. That's about $[Y] per feature. Plus, this is a one-time investment that Jinay will use throughout his career—potentially 5-10+ years."*

**Response Option 3: Offer Alternatives**
*"I completely understand budget considerations. We can:
1. Start with Option A (lower cost) and upgrade later
2. Create a custom package with just essential features
3. Set up a payment plan over 3-4 months
What works best for your budget?"*

---

### If They Need Time to Think:

**Good Response:**
*"Absolutely, this is an important decision. To help with your consideration:
1. I'm offering a 7-day special discount (10% off)
2. I have limited slots this month (creates urgency)
3. What specific concerns can I address to help you decide?
Can we schedule a follow-up call in 2-3 days?"*

---

### If They Want More Features for Same Price:

**Response:**
*"I appreciate your ambition! I've priced these packages based on development time and market rates. However, I can:
1. Offer a discount if you commit today (10% for 7-day booking)
2. Create a custom package mixing features within budget
3. Discuss payment plans to make Premium Package affordable
Which approach interests you most?"*
